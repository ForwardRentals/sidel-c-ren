The SRU and Novacab TESS have been running in the field for years, at real industrial sites, under other people's names. Sidel CREN is what happens when those proven technologies — plus the RPC — are brought together as one platform. Here's where they've already done the work.

Novacab TESS · Bromont, Quebec

The project that proved thermal storage could work like this.

Dr. Stephane Bilodeau's first Novacab TESS deployment ran at an IBM manufacturing facility in Bromont, Quebec — capturing heat the site was already rejecting and banking it as latent energy in phase-change material rather than losing it. The project was significant enough that ASHRAE recognized it with the 2007 International Excellence in Engineering Award, one of the industry's highest honors for applied thermal engineering.

It became the reference project behind everything that followed: roughly 400 Novacab TESS units are now installed across hospitals, high-rises, colleges and government facilities in Canada, all descended from what was proven at Bromont.

The result

IBM saw good energy savings and a steadier, more resilient thermal load — the kind of result that turned a single pilot into an award-winning technology platform. Site-specific figures are available on request; this page describes the deployment and its role in developing the technology, not a certified savings report.

SRU Flue Gas Condenser · Winemaking

Recovering heat at one of California's largest wineries.

Wineries run boilers hard year-round — fermentation temperature control, barrel and tank sanitation, and bottling all draw steam and hot water. E&J Gallo is one of Sidel Systems USA's 100+ industrial clients running the SRU flue-gas condensing economizer on that load, recovering heat — and condensed water — that would otherwise go straight up the stack.

The result

Consistent with the SRU's track record since 1978, Gallo has seen good, ongoing fuel and water savings — the same underlying economics behind the SRU stage of the Sidel CREN platform today.

SRU Flue Gas Condenser · Dairy Processing

The exact thermal profile Sidel CREN targets in dairy.

Darigold's dairy processing runs the same heat-then-cool cycle described on Sidel CREN's own dairy industry page — pasteurization, CIP, drying and refrigeration, all fed by boiler steam and hot water. Darigold has run Sidel Systems USA's SRU on that load, recovering heat before it exits the stack.

The result

Good fuel savings and recovered process water — the same value case Sidel CREN now brings to every large-dairy engagement, integrated with the RPC and Novacab TESS for sites where the full platform fits.

A note on these case studies

Sidel CREN did not exist when these projects ran — the SRU and Novacab TESS were deployed independently by Sidel Systems USA and Smart Phases Inc., the same alliance partners whose technologies now make up the Sidel CREN platform. They're included here because they're the real-world proof behind the platform, not because Sidel CREN itself delivered them.
