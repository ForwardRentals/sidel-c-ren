Half the world's energy

The alliance

Three proven technologies.
One accountable platform.

There are competitors for every individual component. The distinction here is the integration — a mature flue-gas condenser, an authorised particulate-capture technology, and thermal energy salvage, storage and conversion, delivered as a single U.S.–India project architecture with protected IP.

Sidel Systems USA

SRU Flue-Gas Condenser

A condensing economizer and heat-recovery platform for suitable boilers and generators. Recovers sensible and latent heat, condenses water vapour where chemistry permits, and cuts stack losses.

Practised since 1978 · 100+ industrial clients
SAI-DEL / Sidel Systems India

RPC Particulate Collector

The Rotating Particulate Collector for coal, wood, biomass, municipal waste, fuel oil and other particulate-heavy exhaust streams — the missing pillar for solid-fuel operations.

Invented by Parthosarathy Mukherji · U.S. Patent 9,682,340
Smart Phases / Novacab

Novacab TESS

Thermal energy salvage, storage and conversion using synthetic phase change materials. Stores recovered heat, stabilises processes, and converts qualifying heat above ~53–55 °C into behind-the-meter electricity.

Invented by Dr. Stephane Bilodeau, Eng., Ph.D., FEC
