# Sidel CREN

Source for **[sidelcren.com](https://sidelcren.com)** — the site for Sidel CREN, an alliance recovering waste heat from industrial exhaust (dairy, sugar mills, fossil power, steel/metals) and converting it into clean power, water, and greenhouse food production.

Sidel CREN integrates three proven technologies developed by its alliance partners:

- **SRU flue-gas condenser** — [Sidel Systems USA](https://sidelsystems.com) (Sid Abma), in commercial use since 1978
- **RPC rotating particulate collector** — SAI-DEL / Sidel Systems India, patented 2017
- **Novacab TESS thermal energy storage** — Smart Phases Inc., ~400 units installed, award-winning IBM Bromont deployment

Sidel CREN is a DBA of Carbon Resilient Energy NEXUS, Inc., headquartered in Denver, CO.
